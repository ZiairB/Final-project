About the Museum

The Vent Haven Museum is the world's only museum dedicated to the art and history of ventriloquism. Located in Fort Mitchell, Kentucky, the museum preserves the legacy of ventriloquists and their famous figures.

The museum features a large collection of ventriloquist dummies, photographs, books, stage props, and memorabilia. These exhibits help visitors learn how ventriloquism has entertained audiences for many years.

Vent Haven Museum serves as an important resource for preserving the history of ventriloquism and educating visitors about this unique form of performance art.
