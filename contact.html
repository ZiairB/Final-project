Visit the Museum

The Vent Haven Museum is dedicated to preserving the history of ventriloquism through exhibits, artifacts, and collections from famous performers.

Visitors can explore displays of ventriloquist figures, photographs, and memorabilia while learning about the history of this unique art form.

Vent Haven Museum attracts visitors from around the world who are interested in the history of ventriloquism. Guided tours provide an opportunity to learn more about the performers and figures featured in the collection.
