Welcome to the Vent Haven Museum

The Vent Haven Museum is a unique museum dedicated to preserving the art and history of ventriloquism. Located in Fort Mitchell, Kentucky, it is the only museum in the world devoted entirely to this form of entertainment.

Visitors can explore exhibits featuring ventriloquist figures, photographs, books, and memorabilia from famous performers throughout history.

The museum offers an opportunity to learn about the evolution of ventriloquism and the performers who helped make it a popular part of American entertainment.
